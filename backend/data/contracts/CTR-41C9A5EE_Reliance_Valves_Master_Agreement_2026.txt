
        MASTER INDUSTRIAL SUPPLY AGREEMENT
        BETWEEN: Enterprise Procurement Corp ("Buyer")
        AND: Reliance Industrial Valves Ltd ("Supplier", ID: SUP-013)
        
        EFFECTIVE DATE: October 10, 2026.
        TERM: This agreement shall remain in effect for 3 years.
        
        SECTION 1: SCOPE AND SUPPLY OBLIGATIONS
        Supplier shall manufacture and deliver high-precision industrial control valves according to ISO 9001 standards.
        
        SECTION 2: PRICING AND PAYMENT TERMS
        The contracted base price for Model IV-500 shall be INR 12,450 per unit.
        Payment terms are Net 45 days upon verified invoice receipt. An advance payment of 10% is required on formal order placement.
        
        SECTION 3: DELIVERY SLA AND LEAD TIME
        Supplier commits to a guaranteed lead time of 10 business days from purchase order confirmation.
        Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 25 units per production release.
        
        SECTION 4: LIQUIDATED DAMAGES AND PENALTY FOR DELAY
        In the event of unexcused delivery delay beyond the agreed lead time, Supplier agrees to liquidated damages of 1.5% of order value per week of delay, up to a maximum cap of 12%.
        
        SECTION 5: WARRANTY AND DEFECT NOTIFICATION
        Supplier warrants all products against material defects and craftsmanship failures for 36 months from receipt.
        Buyer shall notify Supplier of defective units within 45 days. Supplier must replace all defective units within 7 business days at no cost to Buyer.
        
        SECTION 6: TERMINATION AND RENEWAL
        Either party may terminate for convenience with 60 days advance written notice. Renewal shall require mutual written consent 30 days prior to expiration.
